heygabi.ai

A record of what we actually own — audiobooks, books and board games — kept because we kept losing track.

One question, three shelves

“Do we own this in any format?” — one title, checked against every shelf at once. The page is open to anyone; searching is for household members, so it asks for a Google sign-in.

Not a shop and not a library — nothing here is for sale or for loan.

No tracking and no cookies. The page itself needs no account; the search asks for a sign-in because it looks across the private shelves. Each catalogue handles its own sign-in and will ask before it shows you anything.
