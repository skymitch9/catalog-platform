Estate status

Every pipeline and service, read straight from each one's own public health endpoint — no sign-in, and nothing here shows more than counts and timestamps. A quiet dot, not a dashboard: green means the last check was healthy, amber means it is worth a glance, red means something stopped answering the way it should.

Refreshes on its own every 60s while this tab is open.

Shared index
